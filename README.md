# KhelBharat (खेल भारत) 🇮🇳 - Top 10 Sports Played in India

A modern, responsive, interactive website showcasing the **Top 10 Sports played in India**, including comprehensive information about **legendary players, official rules, ground/court dimensions, and cultural heritage**.

---

## 🌟 Features

1. **Top 10 Indian Sports Covered in Detail**:
   - 🏏 **Cricket** (Pitch: 22 yards, LBW, overs, Sachin, Kohli, Dhoni, Rohit, Kapil Dev)
   - 🤼 **Kabaddi** (Mat: 13m × 10m, Baulk/Bonus lines, 30s raid, Do-or-Die raid, Pardeep Narwal, Pawan Sehrawat)
   - 🏸 **Badminton** (Court: 13.4m × 6.1m, 21-point rally, PV Sindhu, Saina Nehwal, Sat-Chi)
   - 🏑 **Field Hockey** (Turf: 91.4m × 55m, Striking circle D, Major Dhyan Chand, PR Sreejesh, Harmanpreet)
   - ⚽ **Football** (Pitch: 105m × 68m, Offside, Sunil Chhetri, Bhaichung Bhutia, PK Banerjee)
   - ♟️ **Chess** (Chaturanga, 64 squares, double Olympiad gold, Vishy Anand, D Gukesh, Praggnanandhaa)
   - 🏃 **Kho-Kho** (Court: 27m × 16m, 8 sitting chasers, poles, Sarika Kale, Ultimate Kho Kho)
   - 🤼‍♂️ **Wrestling / Kushti** (Mat: 12m × 12m, Akhada mud pit, KD Jadhav, Sushil Kumar, Bajrang, Sakshi, Vinesh)
   - 🏓 **Table Tennis** (Table: 2.74m × 1.525m, 11-point games, Sharath Kamal, Manika Batra, Sreeja Akula)
   - 🏃‍♂️ **Athletics & Javelin** (400m track, 28.96° sector, Neeraj Chopra, Milkha Singh, PT Usha)

2. **Interactive Architectural Ground / Court Visualizer**:
   - Custom SVG diagrams rendered for each sport with exact boundary lines, creases, service boxes, penalty areas, and nets.

3. **5-Tab Deep Dive for Every Sport**:
   - **Overview & Roots**: Cultural significance in India, grassroots culture, team composition.
   - **Ground & Specs**: Length, width, surface, markings, equipment specs.
   - **Rules & Gameplay**: Scoring, match duration, fouls, cards, and unique game mechanics.
   - **Legends & Heroes**: Profiles, titles, eras, stats, and inspiring biographies.
   - **Tournaments & Kit**: Premier domestic leagues (IPL, PKL, ISL, HIL, UKK, UTT), governing bodies (BCCI, BAI, AIFF, etc.), and required kit.

4. **Ground Scale Comparison Tool**:
   - Compare the relative playing surface scale (e.g. Cricket 15,400 m² vs Football 7,140 m² vs Kabaddi 130 m² vs TT Table 4.18 m²).

5. **Interactive Indian Sports Trivia Quiz**:
   - 10-question quiz with instant answers, scoring, and explanations.

6. **Search & Category Filtering**:
   - Real-time search across sport names, player names (e.g. searching "Kohli", "Sindhu", "Neeraj"), and rules.
   - Categories: Team Field, Indigenous, Racket, Mind & Combat, Track & Field, Bookmarked.

7. **Audio Brief (Text-to-Speech)**:
   - Listen to a 30-second audio summary for each sport using browser speech synthesis.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Option 1: Double-Click (Direct in Browser)
Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in **Google Chrome**, **Microsoft Edge**, or any modern browser.

### Option 2: Run with Python Local Server
Open PowerShell in `d:\Yash` and run:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then open: [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)
